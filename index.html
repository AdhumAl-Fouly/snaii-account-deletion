Delete Your Snaii Account

Follow these steps to permanently delete your Snaii account and data.

Steps

  1. Open the Snaii app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the account icon in the top-right corner of the Home screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, then tap Delete My Account.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation box, then tap Delete My Account again to confirm.
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, the following happens immediately:
  • Your personal data, requests, and chat history are permanently deleted.
  • Any active or in-progress requests are canceled immediately.
  • You will need to sign up again to use Snaii in the future.
Need help? Contact us through the Snaii app before deleting your account.
